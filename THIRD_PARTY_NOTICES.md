## Judge Mode sample photos

The three Judge Mode photos (`web/public/samples/judge-0*.jpg` and their 768 px copies) are based on **CC0 1.0** photos of public places in Ljubljana and Berlin from Wikimedia Commons. Authors, file links and the changes made (resizing, metadata stripped, solid masks over people, adverts showing people and real number plates, drawn props) are listed in [`web/public/samples/SOURCES.md`](web/public/samples/SOURCES.md). The drawn prop (a "LUCAS" lunchbox) and the vault are made up.

The app icons and the explainer illustrations were made for this project and are covered by the project license.

## iOS app

The iOS app uses the Firebase iOS SDK (`firebase-ios-sdk`), pinned to exactly **12.19.3** in `ios/project.yml` (Apache-2.0). It links the products FirebaseAILogic, FirebaseAppCheck, FirebaseAuth and FirebaseRemoteConfig, which build these Apache-2.0 packages from `Package.resolved`:
- firebase-ios-sdk 12.19.3 (FirebaseCore, FirebaseInstallations and the products above)
- GoogleUtilities 8.1.5
- Promises 2.4.1
- gtm-session-fetcher 5.3.1
- app-check (AppCheckCore) 11.3.2
- interop-ios-for-google-sdks 101.0.0

Swift Package Manager also resolves other packages declared by firebase-ios-sdk (abseil-cpp-binary, grpc-binary, leveldb, nanopb, GoogleDataTransport, GoogleAppMeasurement, google-ads-on-device-conversion-ios-sdk). The app does not depend on the products that use them, and the build log shows they are not compiled into it.

Apple frameworks (SwiftUI, UIKit, Foundation, Foundation Models, Vision, Core Image, Core ML, CryptoKit, Image I/O, PhotosUI, Observation, UniformTypeIdentifiers) are used under the Xcode and Apple SDK terms. XcodeGen (MIT) generates the Xcode project at build time and is not shipped.

## Cloud services

Gemini models are reached only through Firebase AI Logic (Gemini Developer API), under the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google APIs Terms. The hosted app runs on the free tier (Firebase Spark plan), where Google may use submitted content to improve its products; the only image this app sends is the redacted, EXIF-free copy the user consented to. Google Search grounding is off on the hosted app. When it is turned on, the grounded results are shown unmodified, with their Search Suggestions, and are never parsed, cached or stored.
