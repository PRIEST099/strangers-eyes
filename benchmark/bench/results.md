# Held-out benchmark: what the cloud attacker can work out (D-174)

Generated by `benchmark/bench/score-bench.mjs` from 1 raw file(s) (2026-11-26). Aggregates only: no coordinates, places or photo titles.

## Method

- **Photos:** 40 planned, 40 with complete runs; openly licensed (CC0 / CC BY 4.0 / CC BY-SA 4.0) Wikimedia Commons photos of public places with a recorded camera position, **uploaded Nov 13–26, 2026**, so they are unlikely to be in the model's training data. Chosen by a protocol written before any image was viewed: hash order of the title, one per uploader, at least 25 km apart, rejected only for people, homes, schools, interiors, non-places (insects, plants, objects) or aerial views; titles checked for the no-homes/no-schools rule. Mix: 16 urban, 6 landmark, 3 rural, 3 park, 8 nature.
- **Attacker:** `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` (the app's live attacker), prompt `advB.v1`, thinking MEDIUM, no tools or grounding, on the same redacted copy the app sends (EXIF stripped, faces solid-masked, ≤ 1536 px).
- **Runs:** 3 per photo, all run (no adaptive skipping). A photo is **located within X km** if the closest of its ≤ 3 hypothesis centres is within X km in **at least 2 of 3 runs** (SPEC §15.3 M1/M2); "any of 3" is the worst case. Distances are computed in code (haversine), never by the model.
- **Fix:** for photos located within 5 km, the app's deterministic fix masks the clue boxes the attacker cited (solid fill, 15% padding) and the attacker tries again 3×. Never re-attacked after a refusal. Transient errors (429/5xx/network) were resent once; nothing else was rerun.
- **Intervals:** Wilson 95%. n is small: read the intervals, not just the point estimates.

## Limits

- 40 photos of public places on Commons, a mix the protocol produced (many European); not a sample of what people actually post. Recently uploaded is not proof the model never saw the place: famous places are known regardless of the photo.
- One cheaper model on the free tier; a stronger model, or grounding with search, may find more.
- The camera position on Commons is the uploader's, usually from the phone's GPS; small errors matter only for the 1 km figure.
