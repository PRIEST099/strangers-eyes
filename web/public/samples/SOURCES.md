# Judge Mode sample photos: sources

All three base photos are **CC0 (public domain dedication)** files from Wikimedia Commons. CC0 needs no credit; we credit the authors anyway.
Changes made by this project (D-153): resized to 1600 px and 768 px, metadata stripped, **solid masks** drawn over people, adverts showing people and real number plates, and **drawn props** added (a "City General Hospital" staff badge on judge-02, a "LUCAS" name-tag lunchbox on judge-03). The props, the fake vault and the pins (a park and a square) are made up; no real person, home or school is shown.
